A Self-Taught Programmer

Vinay Bhatt’s journey did not begin in a tech classroom or a prestigious engineering college. It began with curiosity, a computer, and a simple belief—that anyone can learn technology if given the right guidance and opportunity. In 2015, while pursuing a B.Sc. degree with no formal background in computer science, Vinay chose a path less traveled. Instead of enrolling in B.Tech programs or expensive coaching institutes, he taught himself programming using Google, online tutorials, and freely available resources. He designed his own syllabus, studied relentlessly, and slowly built a strong foundation in computer fundamentals, networking, Microsoft Office, and programming. What started as self-learning soon turned into a mission.

Turning Learning into a Movement

Vinay realized that millions of students faced the same challenges he once did—confusing syllabi, expensive courses, and a lack of practical guidance. To solve this, he founded LearnInCreation on April 17, 2018. LearnInCreation was not created as just another EdTech company. It was built as a learning movement — one that breaks traditional barriers and makes technology education simple, structured, and affordable. Today, the platform supports students from beginners to aspiring professionals through free resources, certification programs, and government ISBN–registered books used across multiple countries.

“I didn’t learn programming the traditional way—so I built a platform for those who can’t.” — Vinay Bhatt

Building with Students at the Center

At the heart of LearnInCreation is Vinay’s hands-on approach. Over the years, he has personally conducted 60+ seminars , worked closely with students, and researched learning patterns to understand where traditional education falls short. Every course, book, and certification is designed to answer one question: “Will this actually help a beginner understand and grow?”

This student-first mindset has helped thousands transition from confusion to confidence in programming and IT skills.

Beyond a Startup: Shaping the Future of Education

Vinay’s work extends beyond entrepreneurship. He has spoken at national academic conferences, collaborated with government institutions, and contributed research-driven insights on improving India’s education ecosystem. His efforts have led to discussions with AICTE leadership and policy-level acknowledgment, reinforcing his belief that real change happens when innovation meets responsibility. Currently, Vinay is leading the development of a new social media networking platform , selected as an MVP by FIED at IIM Kashipur, as part of his vision to redefine how people learn, connect, and grow professionally.

Looking Ahead

For Vinay Bhatt, this is just the beginning. His vision for the next decade includes expanding LearnInCreation globally, integrating AI-driven learning models, building next-generation digital platforms, and ensuring that quality education is not a privilege, but a right.

From a self-taught programmer to the founder of a growing EdTech movement , Vinay Bhatt continues to build LearnInCreation with one goal in mind—helping learners believe in themselves and create their own future.
